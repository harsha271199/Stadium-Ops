Aramark Cart Safety Rules
Procedure · OS-7-1 · Form OS-07a-02 · 01/21/22
This Procedure outlines the rules that apply to all persons who operate carts on behalf of Aramark.

Cart Safety Rules:
1. Only employees approved by their direct supervisor may drive carts.
2. All persons who drive electric carts must have a current valid driver's license and Aramark operator's permit.
3. Before employees begin driving carts they are given an orientation to cart operation and the safety rules that are to be followed.
4. Drivers are to slow down or stop at all blind intersections and drive out from doors.
5. Drivers are to drive carefully and courteously and yield to pedestrians or gain the right of way by verbal communication or eye contact.
6. Drivers will conduct Pre-Use Inspection checklist before use each day.
7. Precautions shall be taken to prevent loads from shifting or falling from the cart.
8. Passengers must be in a passenger seat with limbs inside the cart.
9. All accidents shall be reported to the supervisor immediately.
10. Report unsafe conditions to the supervisor immediately.
11. Do not alter the cart's safety equipment.
12. Do not drive while taking medications if the label or your doctor advises against it.
13. After dark always use headlights if the cart is so equipped.
14. There is no "joy riding" or similar non-purposed use.
15. Absolutely no horseplay will be allowed while driving a cart.
16. Take care in where carts are parked so they are not blocking walkways/fire lanes or become an obstacle for people with disabilities.
17. When the rear view is obstructed, do not back up without assistance.
18. Drivers must be responsible, use good judgment, and maintain safe speeds at all times.
19. No unauthorized personnel shall be given rides.
20. These safety rules are to be observed by all employees, not just those who drive carts.
I acknowledge that I have read and received a copy of the Aramark Service Cart Safety Rules and will follow all other applicable rules and regulations.
