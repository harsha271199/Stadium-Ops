# Stadium Ops App — Orientation Demo Script
*For presenting to Student Workers and Concession Bartenders. Keep it simple — show, don't explain everything at once. About 10 minutes.*

---

## Before you start
- Have the app already open on a phone connected to a projector/TV, or have everyone pull it up on their own phone
- Know one real stand name from today's schedule to demo with
- Know your own test login (ID + last name) so you can demo live, not just talk about it

---

## 1. Why this app exists (30 seconds)

**Say:**
> "This app replaces paper checklists and paper inventory sheets. Everything you'd normally write on paper — clocking in, counting stock, reporting a problem — you do here instead. It only takes a minute to learn."

---

## 2. Install it on your phone (2 minutes)

**Say:**
> "First, let's get this on your home screen so you don't have to type the website every time."

**Show — Android:**
1. Open the link in Chrome: `asu-aramark.netlify.app`
2. A banner pops up at the bottom — tap **"Install"**
3. Done — it's now an app icon on your home screen

**Show — iPhone:**
1. Open the link in Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow)
3. Scroll down, tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**

**Say:**
> "Do this now on your own phone before we continue."
*(Give them 2 minutes to actually do it — don't skip this, half the confusion later comes from people never installing it.)*

---

## 3. Logging in (2 minutes)

**Show the login screen. Point to each field as you say it:**

> "Two things to type in — nothing to remember, nothing complicated.
> **ID field**: the last 5 digits of your employee ID.
> **Last Name field**: your real last name, exactly as it's spelled in the system.
> Then tap Sign in."

**Demo it live** with your own test login.

**Say:**
> "Once you're in, you'll see your name, your stand for today, and your shift time — that's automatically pulled from the schedule. If it's wrong or missing, that means the schedule wasn't uploaded correctly yet — tell your supervisor, don't try to fix it yourself."

---

## 4. The home screen — what you'll actually use (3 minutes)

**Show your own home screen after logging in.**

**Say:**
> "This is what you'll see every shift. There's one big red button — that's how you start and end your day."

**Point to and demo:**
- **"Start at this stand"** — tap it to clock in
- **Breaks** — same screen, tap when you go on break, tap again when you're back
- **"Sign out"** at the bottom — only for leaving the app, not clocking out. Don't mix these up.

**If any of them have Portable Stand Access (bartenders at Beer Portable stands, toggle-enabled):**

**Say:**
> "Some of you working the Beer Portable stands will also see a few extra tiles: Checks, Requests, Food Safety. That's because your stand doesn't have its own separate stand lead — you're covering both jobs. Let's look at each one quickly."

- **Checks** — the same opening/closing checklist a stand lead would fill out
- **Requests** — this is how you ask the warehouse for more stock if you're running low
- **Food Safety** — temperature checks, sanitizer log

**Say:**
> "You won't see 'My Team' — that's only for people managing a full crew, not something you need."

---

## 5. Requesting stock, if applicable (2 minutes)

**Only cover this if your group actually has Requests access.**

**Show the Request Stock screen.**

**Say:**
> "If you're running low on something, tap Requests, pick the item, type how many cases you need, and send it. The warehouse gets an alert right away — you don't need to call anyone."

---

## 6. Common questions to head off now

**Say, addressing these directly:**

> - "If the app looks different than what I just showed you, don't worry — just ask, don't guess."
> - "If you can't log in, it's almost always one of two things: you typed the wrong last name spelling, or you're not on today's schedule yet. Either way, find your supervisor, not IT."
> - "Nothing you do in this app affects your actual paycheck — this is for stand tracking only. Still use the normal clock-in machine for your paid hours."

---

## 7. Wrap-up

**Say:**
> "That's really it — clock in, take your breaks, clock out. If you're at a portable stand, you've also got Checks, Requests, and Food Safety. Everything else in the app is for managers and warehouse staff, you won't need it."

**Before they leave:**
- Confirm everyone actually has the app installed on their own phone (not just watched you do it)
- Confirm everyone successfully logged in at least once, live, in front of you
- Give them your contact info for login issues on their first real shift
